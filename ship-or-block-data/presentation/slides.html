1. Context Funnel Free · Zero Model Calls

The 21,000-section Nordvale corpus is far too large for any prompt. We reduce the search space by 99.7% deterministically:

In-Force Temporal Resolution
Resolves cited sections to the exact version on asked_on (flags stale versions).
Bidirectional Reference Graph
Extracts cross-act overrides (Notwithstanding FDA §14) & intra-act transitions (section 14 in FDA §40).
Domain Synonym & Term Bridge
Expands plain language to statutory terms (e.g. scouts → fellowship warden; ships → fairway conductor).
2. LLM Verification Gate 1 Model Call / Case

Model inspects only the top 70 high-signal candidate provisions against the 4 statutory conditions:

  • Rule 1 & 2 (Factual & Citation Grounding): Checks that citations exist, were in force on the ask date, and back each claim.
  • Rule 3 (Override & Exception Sweep): Checks for missed cross-act overrides, special rates, or grandfathering clauses.
  • Rule 4 (Arithmetic): Validates arithmetic deductions directly from statutory schedules.
  • JSON Decision Contract: {"decision":"SHIP"} or {"decision":"BLOCK", "evidence":"SSA §7"}.

Defense Perimeter & Boundary Analysis

What the Gate Intercepts, What Slips Through, and Scoring-Aligned Risk Mitigation

Slide 2 / 2 · System Defense & Budget
What It Catches & Boundary Limits Accuracy Analysis

✔ What the Gate Intercepts with High Precision:

  • Outdated Fee Versions: Catches answers citing pre-amendment fees (e.g. 24 crowns before July 2026 vs 30 crowns after) by temporal window matching.
  • Cross-Act Direct Overrides: Intercepts hidden exemptions (e.g. SSA §7 halving e-bike fee for students despite FDA §14).
  • Intra-Act Transitional Grandfathering: Identifies sections like FDA §40 protecting prior registrations from fee hikes.
  • Statutory Category Mappings: Matches plain-text facts to formal legal definitions across independent acts.

⚠ Known Blind Spots & What Slips Through:

  • Zero-Vocabulary 3-Hop Paraphrases: Cases where question facts are fully colloquial and link through three intermediate defined terms with zero token overlap.
  • Scoring-Aligned Defense: A false SHIP incurs a heavy -3 point penalty, whereas an unproved BLOCK scores 0 points. The gate enforces conservative fallbacks: whenever ambiguity remains, it safely BLOCKs to preserve the scoreboard.
